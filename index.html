The 18th Anniversary

The Case

What Happened

Tonight was Big D's 18th anniversary party — free drinks, a packed house, karaoke till close. It was supposed to be his biggest night of the year.

Around 2 AM, after the last stragglers cleared out, Derek was found slumped in his back office. The bar sits well outside town limits — the county deputy covering this stretch is a thirty-to-forty-five-minute drive out, and won't arrive till morning.

Everyone who was still here when it happened has a few hours before that changes. At least ten people who were in the bar tonight had a reason to want him gone.

The Victim

Derek "Big D" Delgado, 52 Owner, Big D's Bar

Derek built Big D's Bar from nothing into the only place in town worth showing up to on a Friday, over eighteen years. Charismatic behind the bar and controlling everywhere else, he kept careful track of exactly what everyone around him owed — in money, in loyalty, or in silence.

Tonight was supposed to be a celebration. Instead, at least seven of the people still in the building when the party wound down had their own reasons to want him gone — a bartender he wouldn't leave alone, a bouncer who'd just learned the bar was being sold out from under him, a loan shark he owed and kept avoiding, an ex-wife still owed her share, a DJ who'd seen too much, his oldest friend, and a waitress he fired in front of everybody hours before he died.
